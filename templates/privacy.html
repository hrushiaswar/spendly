Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects only the information needed to provide the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to record.
  • Basic technical data — such as session cookies required to keep you signed in.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate Spendly for you: to authenticate your account, store and display your expenses, and generate spending summaries. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our application database. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when the page loads. Any hosting provider we use processes data only as needed to run the service.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
